How this estimate works ＋

GradeMate uses a provisional 5-point scale: A (70–100) = 5, B (60–69) = 4, C (50–59) = 3, D (45–49) = 2, E (40–44) = 1, F (0–39) = 0.

GPA = total of (course units × grade points) ÷ total included units. Failed courses count as zero points and their units stay in the total. Incomplete courses are left out until all fields are entered.

This is an estimate, not an official result. The published scale we found is from UNILAG Distance Learning Institute materials; confirm your programme’s rules with your faculty. Repeats, withdrawals, and excluded courses may be treated differently. Leave out courses that should not count in your GPA.
